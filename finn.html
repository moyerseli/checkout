Shift calendar

“Logged by” on every check-out and return is filled in with whoever is on the shift calendar at that moment. In Google Calendar, make a calendar (for example “Desk shifts”) and add an event for each shift titled with the staff member's name — set it to repeat weekly. Two people on the same shift? Add two events.

  1. In Google Calendar, open Settings and click your shift calendar on the left.
  2. Under Integrate calendar, copy Secret address in iCal format.
  3. In your Google Sheet, open the Settings tab and paste it in cell B2, next to “Shift calendar link”.
The page checks the calendar every 5 minutes. Changes to the calendar can take a few minutes to show up.
